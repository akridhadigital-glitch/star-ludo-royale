# Star Ludo Royale: Privacy Policy

_Effective date: 27 October 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how Star Ludo Royale ("the Game", "we") handles personal data. It is written to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 of India.

## 1. Offline play collects nothing

vs Computer and Pass & Play work without an account and without the internet. Your nickname, settings and saved match stay only on your device.

## 2. What we collect for online play

When you use Play Online, we create an online profile and process:

- A random ID generated by the app (not your phone's hardware ID, IMEI or advertising ID)
- The display name and avatar you choose
- If you link Google: your Firebase/Google account identifier and, at sign-in only, your Google display name. We do not store your email address, photo or contacts.
- Game data: matches played, wins, captures, virtual coins and gems, and match records (room code, players, winner, time)
- Technical data: IP address and connection logs, kept briefly for security and to prevent abuse

## 3. Why we use it

Only to run online matches, keep your progress, show the public leaderboard (display name, avatar, wins and games played), keep the game fair and secure, and answer your requests. We never sell your data, show ads, or use it for profiling or tracking.

## 4. Consent

We process this data on the basis of your consent, given when you choose to use online features. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your online account (Profile → Delete my online account). Offline play remains available.

## 5. Children

Online features are meant for users aged 18 or over, or under-18 users with the verifiable consent of a parent or guardian. If you are a parent and believe your child gave us data without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

## 6. Who processes data for us

Our cloud hosting provider (servers and database) and, if you link Google, Google Firebase Authentication. They act on our instructions and may store data on servers in or outside India, with appropriate safeguards.

## 7. How long we keep it

Your online profile is kept until you delete it. Deleting removes your profile, coins, gems and stats immediately. Past match records keep only an internal ID that no longer links to you. Security logs are kept for a short period.

## 8. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Access to our servers is restricted. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable security safeguards as required by the DPDP Act.

## 9. Your rights

You can access and correct your data (Profile), erase it (Delete my online account), withdraw consent, nominate another person to exercise your rights, and raise a grievance with us. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

## 10. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and show the new version in the app before it applies.
